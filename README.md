# Global Airbnb Market Performance Dashboard | Power BI

## 📌 Overview
This Power BI project analyzes Airbnb performance across 10 major global cities, uncovering market trends, pricing strategies, and guest satisfaction patterns from 2008 to 2020.

## 🎯 Key Questions Explored
- Which cities dominate the Airbnb market?
- How do pricing strategies vary by room type?
- What drives guest satisfaction across different markets?
- How has the listing inventory evolved over time?

## 📊 Dataset
- **Source:** Airbnb internal data (2008–2020)
- **Coverage:** 10 cities including Paris, NYC, Sydney, Mexico City, Rio de Janeiro, Hong Kong, Istanbul, Bangkok, Cape Town, Rome
- **Metrics:** Listings, reviews, pricing, guest ratings (Accuracy, Check-in, Cleanliness, Location, Communication)

## 🔍 Key Insights
- **Market Concentration:** Paris, NYC, and Sydney account for nearly 50% of total listings and 59% of total reviews
- **Pricing Gap:** Hotel rooms average $800/night, while private rooms average $462/night
- **Top Rated Cities:** Mexico City and Rio de Janeiro lead in overall guest satisfaction
- **Improvement Areas:** Cleanliness and Value for Money score lowest across all markets
- **Growth Trend:** Significant listing growth observed from 2008–2016, with decline post-2018
